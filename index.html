隐私政策与使用条款

你的专注记录和时长偏好保存在设备上。专注计时与查看本地记录无需注册账号，也无需联网。

一粒使用 TelemetryDeck 收集带有随机安装标识的基本使用事件，例如应用启动、页面导航和入口点击。专注记录、所选时长和统计历史不会上传至分析服务，也不用于广告或跨 App 追踪。

Privacy policy & terms of use

Your focus records and duration preference are stored on your device. The focus timer and local records work without an account or an internet connection.

Pea uses TelemetryDeck to collect basic usage events with a random installation identifier, such as app launches, screen navigation and button clicks. Focus records, selected durations and statistics history are not uploaded to analytics or used for advertising or cross-app tracking.
